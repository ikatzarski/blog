Hi! I'm Ivan. Welcome to my blog about Software Engineering, DevOps, tech, and who knows what else.

I started this blog to solidify my learning and share knowledge with others. Writing about new concepts helps clarify my understanding and highlights areas I need to improve. That's the goal! Let's see how it goes.

## About Me

I began my education in Economics, but it never sparked my passion. Halfway through the course, I realised it wasn’t for me, but I decided to finish it anyway. Once I graduated, I faced a crossroads: continue in Economics or pursue something I genuinely enjoy.

I've always loved computers and regretted not studying IT from the start. So, I embarked on a master’s degree in Software Development, which I thoroughly enjoyed and excelled in. This set me on my path to becoming a Software Engineer.

Throughout this journey, I noticed that many software problems can be tackled by anyone with basic IT knowledge. These days, knowing how to navigate Stack Overflow and copy-paste code can make you seem like a Software Engineer. But, this oversimplification often leads to questionable code and poor engineering practices. So, how do you start writing good code?

Honestly, I'm still figuring it out. But I believe good code is just one part of being a good Engineer. It’s about more than just writing software; it’s about communicating effectively, listening, expressing your thoughts, being compassionate and patient, sharing knowledge, having an insatiable hunger for learning, and loving problem-solving. There's always something new to learn and improve upon.

That's a bit about me. I hope you find useful insights in my blog.
